Battle Royale

Everyone starts with the same energy. Each round, students face a random classmate on the same question. A wrong or slower answer costs 1 energy. The last hero standing wins.

Questions

Students see each definition and pick its term. The wrong answers are other terms from the set.

New question set

Import from a link: paste the link to a Wayground quiz or a Google Sheet, then click Import.

In the sheet, put the question in column A, the right answer in B and up to 3 wrong answers in C, D and E (a picture link can go in F). A Wayground or Blooket spreadsheet works too.

Or paste rows straight from a sheet (select them, Ctrl+C, then Ctrl+V in the box), one question per line.
